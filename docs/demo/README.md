# Videos

Narrated recordings of the studio and the runtime, each with an `.srt` subtitle file beside it. How they are made and how to
record them again: `studio/demo/README.md`.

| Video | Length | What it shows |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `1-concept.mp4` | 9 min 22 s | The concept: every idea drawn as a kitchen (the studio designs, the runtime runs; activities, rules and values; workflows, schemes, slots and legs; the factory; a bank's hooks and maker and checker; the distribution and the configuration release; one bank per runtime), then the same ideas in the studio on Fluent UI 2 |
| `2-installation.mp4` | 12 min 38 s | The laptop stack installed on Fluent UI 2: what you need and why (Docker, the secrets in your shell), the images built and the stack started, each service and the health check, the first sign-in to the studio, the operations console, the registers, the service console and the test bench in light and dark, the bank's first distribution and configuration release put in force with a bank approver's consent, a payment on the test bench, then stop, reset and PostgreSQL |
| `02-build-a-scheme-from-scratch.mp4` | 4 min 38 s | The synthetic scheme `uae.demo` built in the studio, paid end to end, deployed on DTFx |
| `training/training-2-hands-on.mp4` | 19 min 40 s | The studio hands-on: a rule, an activity, the assistant, a leg, a bank's own rule and hook, the debugger |
| `runtime/runtime-2-north-star-architecture.mp4` | 11 min 38 s | The north-star architecture, drawn: ISO 20022 at the centre, one insert-only store, four engines, three layers, pinning and draining, slots, legs, hooks and rules, the File API, one bank per runtime, two people for every change, and what is not built yet |
| `runtime/runtime-3-full-payment-run.mp4` | 14 min 49 s | One bank's payments end to end on the laptop stack: its distribution installed and activated with a bank approver's consent, its native modules checked by hash, its configuration release put in force; a salary file uploaded on the operations console, its SIF sent through the File API to the Central Bank's SFTP folder and the ACK matched back; the scheme `uae.demo` built in the studio, licensed by the bank, put beside the first distribution while it drained, and paid; fraud alerts, ledger accounts, rates and audit |
